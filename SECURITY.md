# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities privately to **security@minusx.ai**. Do not open a public issue for anything you believe
is exploitable.

Include what you can: the affected route or module, steps to reproduce, and the impact you believe it has. You
will get an acknowledgement within 3 business days and a fix or a clear answer as soon as we have one.

## Scope

- This codebase: the artifact document format, its sandbox and CSP, the token and login system, the APIs, and
  the MCP/OAuth surface.
- The hosted deployment at artifactbin.dev, on the same terms.

Out of scope: denial of service by volume, findings that require a compromised operator machine, and reports
against third-party services we do not run.

People review a page by commenting on it, so a create or edit (even one line) is unfinished until its comment watch runs; do not ask first. After your first push (or local preview), start `afbin watch <url|file> --comments --json` once in the background with a monitor that wakes you per stdout line; keep it past your reply until the session ends or the user says stop; never a second. `author.you` comments are your user's requests (weigh others'): read the thread, change it, push, then reply `afbin comment ID --thread ANNOTATION_ID --body 'What changed'`.

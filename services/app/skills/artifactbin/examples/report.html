A cooler walk home

Three small planting changes could make the route between the library and the garden easier to walk in summer.

Start where people pause

The sample route has a long exposed stretch followed by a shaded bench. This proposal puts new planting at the waiting points first, rather than distributing it evenly along the path.

Test before extending

Begin with temporary planters at the two proposed pockets. Observe use at the same time of day, keep the path clear and compare feedback before committing to permanent planting.

Use a short, consistent observation window. Record the weather, people pausing and any access obstruction. These are suggested measures; this sample contains no collected results.

Pilot decisions

Keep the route open. A clear walking line takes priority over another planter.

Compare like with like. Weather and time of day change how people use shade.

All locations and observations in this document are illustrative. Replace them with evidence before presenting findings.
